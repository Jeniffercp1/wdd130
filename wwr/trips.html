Featured Trips

Half-Day River Run

A friendly beginner route with calm practice water, lively rapids, and time for photos. This trip is ideal for families and first-time paddlers.

Canyon Rush

A full-day adventure with stronger rapids, canyon views, and a riverside lunch. This route works well for groups who want a balanced challenge.

Our most intense guided route includes technical paddling, fast water, and advanced teamwork. Previous rafting experience is recommended.
